Emberline is a top-down arena survival game: hold the line through 10 waves of the cinder swarm. Move with WASD or arrows, aim with the mouse, hold click or Space to fire, and press E to unleash a nova. Clear a wave to draft one of three upgrades, chain kills to raise your score multiplier, and gather the embers the swarm drops to repair your hull.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests: the game is static files under game/.
